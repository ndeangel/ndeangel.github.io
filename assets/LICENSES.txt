Institution logos in the "Education & Experience" timeline (assets/img/logo_cern.svg, logo_unige.webp, logo_inaf.webp)

         (github.com/zenodo/zenodo, zenodo/modules/theme/static/img/cern.svg), recoloured to CERN blue and
         cropped to a circle by the page.
  UNIGE: the seal of the University of Geneva, cut from the logo on the slides "Geneva_Young_researchers_day_2022".
  INAF:  the symbol of the INAF logo, cut from the slides "COSPAR2024_CUSP".
  These are trademarks of CERN, the University of Geneva and INAF, not covered by any licence of this website.
  CERN's guidelines ask third parties to obtain CERN's approval before using its logo; the other two institutions
  publish their own usage rules. To remove a logo, delete the `logo:` line of its entry in _data/timeline.yml
  (and the file in assets/img/).
